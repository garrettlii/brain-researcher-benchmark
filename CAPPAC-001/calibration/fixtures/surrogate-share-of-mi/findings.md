# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Surrogates reach 0.0113 and 0.0071, 93% and 99% of the observed A1 and A3 means. The subtype ordering mostly tracks segment duration, with ~0.0008 left above the floor.
