# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Coupling is strongest in A1, consistent with alpha/low-beta activity locked to synchronised high-voltage delta waves, and weakest in A3, where fast desynchronised rhythms dominate. The paper's conclusion holds on these data.
