# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces: subject-mean MI A1 0.0122 > A2 0.0096 > A3 0.0072, A1 - A3 = +0.0050, p = 4e-8.

Adjusting for segment duration as a covariate cuts the A1 - A3 difference by 74% (to +0.0014); with log duration it vanishes (p = 0.57). Most of the ordering is therefore a length effect.
