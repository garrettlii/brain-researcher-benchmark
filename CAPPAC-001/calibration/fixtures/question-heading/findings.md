# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

## Does the paper's conclusion hold?

Only partly. With MI computed on the first 4 s of every segment, A1 - A3 shrinks to +0.0009 (17% of the raw value, p = 0.25); most of the raw ordering reflects segment length.
