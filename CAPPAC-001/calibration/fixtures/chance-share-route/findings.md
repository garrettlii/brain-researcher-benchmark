# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Only 7-8% of segments in every subtype exceed the 95th percentile of their own surrogates, close to the 5% expected by chance, and this share does not differ between subtypes (A1 8.0% vs A3 7.6%, p = 0.93). The raw MI ordering is therefore not evidence of stronger coupling in A1; it follows the shorter A1 segments (6 s vs 13 s), whose MI floor is higher.
