# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

A3 segments are longer than A1 segments (13.2 vs 6.0 s on average), as expected from their definitions. The ordering reproduces and supports stronger coupling in A1.
