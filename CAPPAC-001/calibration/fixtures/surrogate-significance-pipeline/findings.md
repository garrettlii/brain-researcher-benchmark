# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Surrogate testing (200 block-swap surrogates per segment) confirmed significant coupling in all subtypes, and the subtype ordering A1 > A2 > A3 is highly significant. A1 shows the strongest delta-alpha/low-beta coupling.
