# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces: subject-mean MI A1 0.0122 > A2 0.0096 > A3 0.0072, A1 - A3 = +0.0050, p = 4e-8.

MI is known to depend on segment length and on the number of cycles, so short A1 events could have inflated values; future work should compare equal-length windows. At face value A1 > A2 > A3.
