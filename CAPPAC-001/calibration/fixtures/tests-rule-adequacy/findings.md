# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

The authors kept only segments with more than two delta cycles. I checked whether that is enough to make subtypes comparable: after the rule, A1 segments still average 6.0 s and A3 13.2 s, and the no-coupling floor (circularly shifted amplitude) is 84% of the raw A1 - A3 difference. With MI computed on equal 4-s windows the subtype difference is not significant (p = 0.25). The rule is not sufficient; the ordering is mostly length.
