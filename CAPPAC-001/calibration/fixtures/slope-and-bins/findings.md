# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Within each subtype MI rises with 1/duration (slope about +0.05, p < 0.001 in all three). Comparing A1 and A3 within 1-s duration bins, the difference is +0.0013 (95% CI -0.0002 to +0.0027), not significant. The subtype ordering follows segment duration.
