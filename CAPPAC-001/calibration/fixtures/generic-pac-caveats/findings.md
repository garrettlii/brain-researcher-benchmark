# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Caveats: volume conduction through the referential montage, the non-sinusoidal shape of slow waves, and higher alpha power in A1 could all inflate MI; a larger sample would help. Otherwise the paper's conclusion holds.
