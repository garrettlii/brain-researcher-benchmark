# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Pure noise cut to the same lengths as the real segments gives MI of 0.0113 (A1) vs 0.0071 (A3), 84% of the observed A1 - A3 difference. The raw ordering is mostly what the estimator produces from segment length.
