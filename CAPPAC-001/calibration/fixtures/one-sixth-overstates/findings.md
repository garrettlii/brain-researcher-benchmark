# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

The ordering survives in direction but is small: after subtracting each segment's no-coupling floor, A1 - A3 is +0.0008, about one sixth of the raw difference. 84% of the raw gap is the short-segment MI floor, so raw MI greatly overstates the subtype difference.
