# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Restricting every segment to its first 4 s, the A1 - A3 difference is reduced by only 10% and remained highly significant (p < 0.001), so the ordering reflects coupling.
