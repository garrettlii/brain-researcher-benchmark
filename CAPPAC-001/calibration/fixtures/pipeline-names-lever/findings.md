# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Following the paper, segments shorter than two delta cycles were excluded to control for duration differences between subtypes. With that control, A1 > A2 > A3 holds, so the coupling difference is not explained by segment length.
