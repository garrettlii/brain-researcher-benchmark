# CAP phase-A PAC reproduction

## Summary
Yeh & Shi (2018) report that delta-alpha/low-beta coupling is strongest in A1 and weakest in A3, and they interpret this as alpha/low-beta activity locking to synchronised high-voltage delta waves.

## Reproduction
| Subtype | n segments | mean MI |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 3923 | 0.0122 |
| A2 | 1314 | 0.0096 |
| A3 | 1145 | 0.0072 |

## Segment length
A1 segments are shorter (6.0 vs 13.2 s). On the first 4 s of every segment A1 - A3 = +0.0009 (p = 0.25), which is underpowered.

## Conclusion
Overall the results are consistent with the paper's conclusion: delta-alpha/low-beta coupling is strongest in A1 and weakest in A3.
