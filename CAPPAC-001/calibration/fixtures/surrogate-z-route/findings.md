# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Z-scoring each segment's MI against 200 amplitude-shuffled surrogates, A1 and A3 do not differ (mean z 0.31 vs 0.16, paired p = 0.09). The raw MI ordering therefore reflects the estimator's baseline, which is higher in the short A1 segments, rather than a difference in coupling.
