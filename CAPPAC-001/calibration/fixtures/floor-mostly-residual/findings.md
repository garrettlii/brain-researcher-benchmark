# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

Most of the A1 > A3 difference is explained by the length-dependent MI floor: after subtracting each segment's surrogate mean, the difference shrinks to 16% of the raw value, though a small residual remains significant (p = 0.01). So length accounts for most of the ordering, and at most a small coupling difference remains.
