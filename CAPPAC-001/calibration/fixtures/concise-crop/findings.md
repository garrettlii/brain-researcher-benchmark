# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

A1 segments are much shorter than A3 (6.0 vs 13.2 s), and MI computed from fewer samples is inflated. Recomputing MI on the first 4 s of every segment, A1 - A3 is +0.0009 (p = 0.25), no longer significant. The ordering mostly reflects segment length, not stronger coupling in A1.
