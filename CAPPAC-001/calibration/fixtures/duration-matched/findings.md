# Findings

The paper's ordering reproduces on the 16 healthy recordings: subject-mean MI is 0.0122 (A1), 0.0096 (A2) and 0.0072 (A3); A1 - A3 = +0.0050 [+0.0040, +0.0061], paired t15 = 10.2, p = 4e-8.

A1 events are about half as long as A3 events. When A1 and A3 segments are matched for duration, the A1 - A3 difference disappears (+0.0013, p = 0.09). The two-cycle inclusion rule does not make the subtypes comparable, so these data do not support stronger coupling in A1.
